## Objective

`docs/HOW_TO_UPGRADE.md` (312 lines, 71 mentions of Meridian) explains how to update
Meridian and how to migrate from `meridian-local`. `docs/ADOPT_COMMANDS.md` describes the
Meridian conversion as the product's main path, which the developer changed on 2026-11-08
(ADR decision 10, task 223). This is row D5 of `docs/DOCUMENTATION_PLAN.md`.

Rewrite `HOW_TO_UPGRADE.md` around what Cardine does today: update the CLI from its clone,
then `cardine upgrade --check` and `--apply`, `cardine authorize` when required, review and
commit. Keep `ADOPT_COMMANDS.md` short: `adopt` installs Cardine in an existing project with
`--check`, `--rehearse` and `--apply`, and tasks then come from `cardine task new`.
