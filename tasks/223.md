## Objective

On 2026-11-08 the developer decided how the two private projects move to Cardine and what 0.9
is. `docs/ADR_CARDINE_1_0.md` still says the opposite in a few places, and
`docs/DOCUMENTATION_PLAN.md` still lists DD3 as undecided. This task records the decisions.
It changes no code.

The decisions:

- `cardine adopt` installs Cardine in an existing project. It does not translate task
  records; tasks come from `cardine task new`.
- Contoso and Fabrikam, the only Meridian users, convert through a one-shot preparation
  outside the product: their open records are rewritten by hand in Cardine format.
- After the last conversion, Meridian support is removed from `adopt` and `cardine.legacy`.
- 0.9 is a `v0.9.0` Git tag, installed from a clone. PyPI starts at 1.0.
- The stability promise starts at Cardine 1.0.0 (DD3).
