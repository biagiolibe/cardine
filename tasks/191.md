## Objective

`cardine adopt` stops a conversion with `ADOPT_RECORD` when an open record depends on a
task whose row the previous workflow already moved to `tasks/QUEUE_ARCHIVE.md`. The
switch of this repository hit it on 2026-11-07:

```text
"diagnostics": ["ADOPT_RECORD"], "outcome": "blocked", ...
"plan": {"diagnostics": ["178: unknown dependencies: 164"] ...}
```

Task 164 was closed, and its row was archived when its phase closed. The coordinator
had to edit record 178 by hand to remove the dependency. Every project that archives
closed phases, as the previous workflow tells it to, meets the same stop at its
conversion; the next private consumer (Contoso) is one.

`read_legacy_project` in `src/cardine/legacy/conversion_reader.py` builds
`closed_ids` from the rows of `tasks/QUEUE.md` only. Read the closed rows of
`QUEUE_ARCHIVE.md` next to the queue with the same row reader, and count them as
closed. An archived row stays out of `rows`, so it is never converted, reported as a
duplicate, or carried into the new queue.
